Letter labeller

Load label_review.json. Cuts are polylines — drag the square handles (top/bottom independently for slant, middle for curve). Labels auto-fill from the spelling. Move: drag handle · drag a cut's line (not a handle) to slide the whole cut · dbl-click (or ⌥-click) a cut to add a point · shift+click a handle to delete. +Cut: click to drop a new cut. Inpaint: paint a mask over non-letter ink, then Run inpaint to reconstruct the paper. ⌘space next · ⏎ done+next · s skip · ←/→ move · ⌘Z undo · ⌘⇧Z redo.
